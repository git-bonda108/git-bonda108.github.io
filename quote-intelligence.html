Quote Intelligence: every competitor quote, reconciled to the cent.

A sales consultant sits beside a customer and gets one question: how does this compare to the other builder's quote? Quote Intelligence answers it in six to eight minutes instead of half a day, and refuses to invent numbers when the source doesn't say so.

Deliverables

An editable web comparison table, a branded Excel workbook and a Word document in the company's review template, all rendered from one data model.

The problem

Off-the-shelf LLMs fall over at exactly the point that matters: the numbers.

Sales consultants at premium home builders lose half a day per customer producing quote comparisons by hand. The competitor quote arrives in a different format, uses different scope language for the same feature, omits quantities on "included" items and pads with promotional discounts that expire mid-cycle.

Models invent prices that sound plausible, sum figures that don't reconcile against source PDFs, and drift on rounding across quotes totalling half a million dollars. In this domain a single fabricated $500 can lose the sale.

The problem, put precisely: extract structured line items from unstructured PDFs, align them across two builders' vocabularies, and reconcile the numbers penny-perfectly, while making it impossible for the model to guess.

Three walls against fabrication

Built from different ingredients, so a single bug cannot let a figure through.

A figure has to pass all three walls to reach the consultant. Anything that fails is rewritten to Unconfirmed: an amber tint, no value in the totals, a flag in the ambiguity list.

Wall 1evidence or flagstructural · enforced by the output schema

Every priced row must carry a source page and a verbatim snippet containing the figure. The agents cannot emit a price without emitting evidence. The same rule applies to manual and chat edits at the API layer.

Wall 2cross-model verifierindependent · different model, temperature, prompt

A second model re-reads both PDFs and confirms every figure. Because it runs on a different model, it is unlikely to hallucinate the same figure. Any row it cannot confirm is demoted.

Wall 3pure-code substring checkdeterministic · no model involved

Does the emitted snippet, whitespace-normalised, occur literally on the cited page? If not, the row becomes Unconfirmed, and Unconfirmed is sticky: only an explicit human action clears it, and that action has to pass Wall 3 too.

The math never liesThe compute layer uses Decimal arithmetic throughout. When any row is Unconfirmed, section subtotals become None and the headline blocks at $0 with an explicit note. Silence is unacceptable; blocking is honest.

Evaluation

Sixty-three assertions, four suites, every change.

Every suite is self-verifying: on the first failure it prints the exact assertion that broke and exits non-zero. Because the compute engine and tripwire are pure code, any recorded comparison can be replayed offline against the current engine.

Verified against a live job

A real customer job with both quote PDFs text-extractable: 21 sections, 57 rows, 26 ambiguities raised for review. The headline correctly blocked at $0 because unresolved ambiguities were present, with an explicit note telling the consultant why.

Two further jobs with scanned, image-only PDFs: without the OCR flag they surface a specific 422; with it, the vision fallback transcribes each page in about ten seconds and the pipeline runs end to end.

Production hardening

  • Federated identity; the application never sees a password
  • IDOR guard: a non-owner gets 404, not 403, so session existence is not leaked
  • Zero secrets in code; managed-identity credential chain
  • No customer PII; comparisons keyed by project address; a persistent INTERNAL banner on every export
  • SSRF guard on every outbound fetch; six-layer upload validation
  • Reconnect-safe SSE with sequence numbers; reasoning heartbeats every ~10 s
  • Append-only audit of every field-level edit, seven-year retention

Humans where the money is

The consultant resolves every ambiguity before totals unlock, and only an explicit human action clears an Unconfirmed row, an action that itself has to pass the tripwire. Routine rows need no touch; the system asks only about what the source genuinely does not say.

Failure modes, designed for

  • Hallucinated prices — three walls from different ingredients; 63 assertions, zero fabricated figures
  • Injection via a quote PDF or a design page — PDFs are data; pages are fetched under the SSRF guard and never executed
  • Scanned, image-only PDFs — a specific 422 by default; opt-in vision OCR, about ten seconds a page
  • Over-reliance — every cell is source-linked and inline-editable; an INTERNAL banner on every export

Observability and replay

  • SSE stream reports every agent handoff; reconnect-safe with sequence numbers and reasoning heartbeats
  • Because compute and tripwire are pure code, any recorded comparison replays offline against the current engine
  • Self-verifying suites print the exact assertion that broke and exit non-zero
